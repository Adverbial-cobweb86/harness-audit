## 📊 What Does "Context" Mean?

Think of "context" like your AI assistant's **working memory**. Just like you can only hold so many things in your mind at once, AI tools have limits too. When they have to read a cluttered project, they use up more memory just figuring out what's important.

harness-audit measures:
- **Before**: How much memory your AI needs just to understand your messy project
- **After**: How much memory it needs after we've cleaned everything up

You'll see impressive numbers like **"Reduced context usage by 43%!"** - That means your AI can think more clearly and give you better answers!
